Platmark

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Platmark is made by Ouroboros Technologies ("we"). The short version: we don't collect any data about you. Platmark has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device, and which other services it talks to on your behalf.

Your maps

The maps you make, including their notes, photos and image overlays, are files stored where you choose: on your device, or in iCloud Drive or another file provider. We never receive a copy. iCloud Drive is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Location

If you allow it, Platmark uses your location while the app is open to show where you are, to place markers at your position, and to record a track you start. Your location is only written into a map when you add something there, and it is never sent to us. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings (iPhone and iPad) or System Settings (Mac).

Photos

Platmark only reads the photos you pick or drop into a map. A photo's embedded location and direction, if it has them, are used to place it. The photo is saved inside your map file.

Map tiles and data layers

To draw the map, Platmark downloads map tiles and data directly from their publishers, including OpenFreeMap, EOX, Esri (ArcGIS Online and Living Atlas), Regrid, Macrostrat, the US Geological Survey, NOAA, the US Census Bureau, the US Fish and Wildlife Service, the Bureau of Land Management, and several state agencies. Like any website you visit, these servers receive your IP address and the area of the map being requested, and they handle that information under their own privacy policies. Platmark sends them nothing about you or your maps. Recently viewed tiles are cached on your device, and you can empty the cache in the Mac's Settings.

Place search

Searching for a place or address uses Apple Maps. Your search text and the visible map area are sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

The Unlimited Elements purchase is handled entirely by the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether this account owns the unlock.

Children

Platmark is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, bug reports and suggestions are welcome. Email support@ouroborostechnologies.com and include your device, its system version, and the Platmark version (shown under About Platmark). If something went wrong with a particular map, attaching the file helps.

Common questions

How many elements can a map hold for free?

Ten markers, paths and areas per map. Every feature works on a free map. Opening or importing a larger file is never limited; only adding to it is.

What is Platmark Pro?

A one-time purchase that removes the limit on every map, and includes every future Pro feature. There is no subscription. It works on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, and can be shared with Family Sharing. To get it, open a map, go to the Document tab and tap Get Platmark Pro.

I bought Platmark Pro on another device. How do I get it here?

On iPhone and iPad, open a map, go to the Document tab and tap Restore Purchase. On the Mac, it is in Platmark ▸ Settings and in the Document tab. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Where are my maps saved?

Maps are ordinary files. You choose where they go, on your device or in iCloud Drive, from the document browser on iPhone and iPad or the Save dialog on the Mac. Platmark keeps no copy anywhere else.

Can I open my maps in other apps?

Yes. A .pmark map is a package holding a plain GeoJSON file (named map.geojson) beside any photos and image overlays. You can also export to GeoJSON, KML, KMZ, GPX or CSV, or as an image or PDF.

Why is a data layer blank?

Data layers come straight from their publishers' servers, mostly US federal and state agencies, and cover the United States. A layer is blank outside the area it covers, at zoom levels its publisher doesn't serve, or while its server is down. Each layer's information button shows who publishes it and where it covers.

Does Platmark work offline?

Your maps, drawing, measuring and GPS all work without a connection. Map tiles you have recently viewed are cached for a while, but Platmark does not download areas for offline use, and new areas need a connection to show the base map and data layers.
